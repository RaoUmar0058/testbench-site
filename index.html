SERVICES

Testing that fits wherever you are in development

SEV-1

Bug hunting

Systematic passes through your build to find crashes, soft-locks, and breaking bugs, each one logged with steps to reproduce.

SEV-2

Compatibility testing

SEV-1

Performance checks

Frame rate, load times, and memory behavior under real play, flagged against the platform's own requirements.

SEV-3

Regression passes

Re-testing after a patch to confirm fixed bugs stayed fixed and nothing new broke in the process.

SEV-3

Store readiness

A pass against platform certification checklists before you submit to storefronts like Steam or console stores.

PROCESS

How a testing pass runs

01

Scope the pass

You tell me the build, platform, and what worries you most. I put together a short test plan and a quote before anything starts.

02

Test the build

I play with intent — following the plan, but also breaking things a normal player would stumble into by accident.

03

Log everything

Every issue gets a severity, reproduction steps, and video or screenshots where useful, so your team can act without guesswork.

04

Deliver & debrief

You get the full report plus a short call to walk through anything that needs context.
